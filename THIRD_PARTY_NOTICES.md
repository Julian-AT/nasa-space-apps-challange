# Third-party notices

## ShadowAstra_Atlas

Visual language (design tokens, palette, layout ideas) and small code fragments in `apps/web`
are adapted from ShadowAstra_Atlas, https://github.com/ABRUBAB/ShadowAstra_Atlas. Each adapted
file carries a header comment naming it.

The Maps page (`/explore/earth`, `/explore/moon`, `/explore/mars`) is ShadowAstra's Atlas page,
copied with the authors' permission: `apps/web/components/shadowastra/atlas/**` (their
`src/pages/Atlas.tsx`, map, three and ui components), `apps/web/lib/shadowastra-atlas/**` (their
`src/data` and `src/lib` modules), their scoped stylesheet in `apps/web/app/shadowastra-atlas.css`,
and their published data, globe textures, place thumbnails and logo in
`apps/web/public/shadowastra/{data,textures,thumbs,brand}`. Their site ratings, scores, twins and
grid values come from the ShadowAstra pipeline and are labelled as such on the page; Terraluna's
own pins, evidence cards and hillshade on that page come from the Terraluna release bundle. The
textures and imagery keep the NASA/ESA/USGS credits ShadowAstra lists in its layer records.
