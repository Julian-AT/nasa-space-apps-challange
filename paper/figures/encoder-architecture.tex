\caption{Encoder architecture, drawn from input (bottom) to loss (top). A ResNet-18 layout at
half width maps a \ConfigInputPx{} by \ConfigInputPx{} window with three channels to a
\ConfigEmbeddingDim{} value embedding $h$. The grey frame is the repeated BasicBlock, two per stage
in four stages. Three heads train the backbone: a projector for the VICReg loss on two views of one
window, a physics head that regresses the fingerprint of Section~\ref{sec:fingerprint} with a
masked loss, and a body head behind a gradient reversal layer, through which $\lambda$ acts. Only
the backbone with the L2 normalisation (dashed) is exported to ONNX; the heads are used in
training only. The stride 2 stem is a recorded deviation from the specified stride 1.}
\label{fig:encoder}
