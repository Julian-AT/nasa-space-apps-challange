\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{Scale range.} The fingerprint covers lags of \ConfigLags\,m inside
\ConfigWindowM\,m windows on a \ConfigGridM\,m grid. It says nothing about the decimetre to metre
roughness that governs walking, driving or radar scattering, and the Hurst exponent describes only
that range of lags. Roughness is scale dependent \citep{shepard2001,kreslavsky2013}, so agreement
at hectometre lags does not imply agreement at the scale of a boot or a wheel, and no continuous
roughness spectrum is measured. No contact scale property, such as block size, soil strength or
trafficability, is inferred.

\paragraph{The lunar target is largely interpolated.} For only \MoonTargetObservedPct\% of the target window's valid analysis cells does the nearest 5 m pixel hold a LOLA return; elsewhere the heights are the provider's interpolation between
tracks \citep{barker2021}. The fraction of \ConfigGridM\,m analysis cells that contain a return
anywhere inside the cell is not available, because only the nearest product pixel was checked, and the
short lag statistics of the target depend on the interpolation. The
provider's error clones measure this uncertainty under the published error model; they were
propagated to the shortlist distances, but not to the background or to the encoder.

\paragraph{Earth surface model.} Copernicus GLO-30 is a digital surface model, so canopy and buildings are part of the height \citep{airbus2022copernicus}, and so is any snow present when the elevation was acquired. The land cover mask keeps windows that are mostly bare or sparse, grassland, shrubland, or snow and ice, for at least \ConfigMinAllowedPct\% of their analysis cells, so a retained window can still contain other classes, low vegetation is kept, and the mask is a single year of a land cover product at
the analysis cell centres. Surface change since the elevation was acquired, seasonal snow and
moisture were not checked.

\paragraph{Mars.} The Martian elevation models are stereo products of the Ames Stereo Pipeline \citep{beyer2018}, and their quality varies between models; models are filtered by their CTX minus MOLA height differences, and stereo
artefacts that survive the filter can mimic roughness. The Martian elevation models have no published error clones, so the Martian distances carry no measurement error spread, and the two Martian target sites each rest on a single CTX model.

\paragraph{Radar.} The radar comparison was not completed. The Mini-RF mosaic mixes viewing
geometries and states no transmit handedness, the UAVSAR handedness convention is not verified
against Mini-RF, and no radar pair has passed the eligibility check of band, convention, geometry and support. Even a completed comparison would face a band difference and the
ambiguity that CPR is not monotonic with rock abundance \citep{riveravalentin2026}.

\paragraph{Search universe and independence.} Two regions were searched, and overlapping windows
on a half window stride are not independent samples. A short distance makes an Earth window a
candidate for interpreting the lunar signature under these observables, not a confirmed analog,
and a window that was not evaluated is not a negative. For the lunar target the only shortlisted
window lies farther from the target than any other window of the target site and is not a close
analog; any analog statement in this paper is limited to terrain statistics at the stated lags.

\paragraph{The encoder.} The encoder was trained on only \DatasetWindows{} windows at the reduced
scope of Section~\ref{sec:training}, and it did not meet its acceptance criteria (\GFiveVerdict). An
untrained copy of the network is a strong baseline on these probes. The validation sweep of
Section~\ref{sec:benchmarks} chose the probe penalty on the scored rows and rests its seed
stability on one target window. On test blocks the encoder's physics probe passes, but its body probe, agreement and seed
stability fail, and the morphology probe and the image baselines are not available. A run with a
held out body is not available either. The
encoder is reported as experimental, labelled Predicted, and kept out of every ranking. Its probes
test agreement with our own fingerprint; they do not show that it captures geology.
