\section{Embedding visualisations}
\label{sec:appendix-embeddings}

The planned visualisations of the encoder are a UMAP projection \citep{mcinnes2018} of test block
embeddings coloured by body, the same projection for the run without gradient reversal, galleries
of the nearest Earth neighbours of each target with their hillshades, and the twenty largest
disagreements between the encoder and the fingerprint as failure cases. They are drawn only from
the embedding export of a frozen model, and the encoder promotion gate is \GateFiveStatus. No
embedding export exists in this build, so no visualisation is shown; none is drawn from an
untrained or mock model.
