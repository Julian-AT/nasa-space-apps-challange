\section{Embedding visualisations}
\label{sec:appendix-embeddings}

All values in this appendix are Predicted, from the experimental encoder, and none enters a ranking.

\paragraph{Nearest Earth neighbours of the lunar target.} The evaluation record lists the encoder's five
nearest Earth windows to the lunar target among the encoder's own test windows. Their cosine
distances run from \GalleryOneDist{} to \GalleryFiveDist, and the nearest lies at latitude
$\GalleryOneLat$ and longitude $\GalleryOneLon$ degrees, in the Copernicus tile that also holds the
Rosamond region. The deterministic ranking was not an input of that evaluation, so the report gives
no side by side comparison with the fingerprint shortlist.

\paragraph{Failure cases.} The evaluation record lists the \FailureCount{} largest disagreements between the
encoder's ranking of Earth windows for the lunar target and the ranking by the root mean square
difference of the ten $\ln \nu$ values. Their rank differences run from
\FailureMinDisagreement{} to \FailureMaxDisagreement{} places, so windows that are close to the
target in the fingerprint can be far from it in the embedding and the reverse, which is the same
weak agreement that the physics agreement test of Section~\ref{sec:results-ml} measures. A fraction
of \OodFraction{} of the pooled test windows lies beyond the ninety ninth percentile of the
Mahalanobis distance of the training embeddings, and the record keeps \OodExamples{} of them as
out of distribution examples.

\paragraph{Not shown.} The UMAP projections \citep{mcinnes2018} of test embeddings coloured by body,
with and without gradient reversal, and hillshade galleries of the neighbours need an embedding
export that was not produced, so they are not drawn.
